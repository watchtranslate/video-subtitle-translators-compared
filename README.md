## Which YouTube subtitle translator to use?

Sometimes YouTube's auto-translate still doesn't make sense, and sometimes the video has no captions at all. Before switching tools, check whether the new one solves the problem you actually have.

**A few YouTube subtitle translation tools**

|  | Videos with no subtitles | Beyond YouTube | Pricing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **WatchTranslate** | Listens to the audio and creates subtitles as you watch[^wt-supported] | X, Bilibili and other video sites, plus local files; the Android app has a built-in browser for web videos[^wt-supported][^wt-local][^wt-app] | 20 free points on sign-up, about 20 minutes; then points per minute; free when someone has already translated the same video[^wt-pricing] |
| **YouTube's own** | Some videos have no auto captions; auto captions are machine-generated and quality varies[^yt-auto-captions] | YouTube only[^yt-auto-captions] | Free[^yt-auto-captions] |
| **Immersive Translate** | AI subtitles need a Pro membership[^it-video] | Other video platforms[^it-video] | AI subtitles are part of the Pro membership[^it-video] |
| **Trancy** | AI subtitles are YouTube only and need Premium[^trancy-faq] | Some streaming and course platforms[^trancy-subtitles] | AI subtitles need Premium[^trancy-faq] |
| **Language Reactor** | Cannot create subtitles from audio on YouTube[^lr-forum-asr] | Netflix[^lr-store] | Free to install, with in-app purchases[^lr-store] |

## Immersive Translate alternatives for videos

Before choosing a video translation tool, check whether it can handle videos with no subtitles, whether it works on your phone, and whether it can translate movies stored on your computer. The common options each suit a different way of watching.

**Ways to get subtitles when watching videos**

|  | Videos with no subtitles | Where it works | Who it suits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **WatchTranslate** | Listens to the audio and creates subtitles as you watch[^wt-supported] | YouTube, X, Bilibili and other sites, plus local files; the Android app has a built-in browser for web videos[^wt-supported][^wt-local][^wt-app] | Anyone who wants to understand foreign-language videos, including ones with no subtitles[^wt-supported] |
| **Trancy** | AI subtitles are YouTube only and need Premium[^trancy-faq] | YouTube and some streaming and course platforms[^trancy-subtitles] | Saving words and learning a language[^trancy-learning] |
| **Language Reactor** | Cannot create subtitles from audio on YouTube[^lr-forum-asr] | Netflix and YouTube in desktop Chrome[^lr-store] | Learning a language with Netflix[^lr-store] |
| **YouTube's own captions** | Some videos have no auto captions; auto captions are machine-generated and quality varies[^yt-auto-captions] | YouTube only[^yt-auto-captions] | Videos that already have captions[^yt-auto-captions] |

## Immersive Translate or WatchTranslate?

Some foreign-language videos come with subtitles, some only have sound, and some are movies sitting on your computer. Which tool fits depends on how you usually watch.

**Videos without subtitles, local files and pricing**

|  | WatchTranslate | Immersive Translate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Videos with no subtitles** | Listens to the audio and creates subtitles; works on YouTube, X, Bilibili and other video sites[^wt-supported] | AI subtitles need a Pro membership[^it-video] |
| **Video files on your device** | Open local files in the browser on a computer or phone, no install needed, files not uploaded; the Windows and Mac apps support MKV, AVI, HEVC and more[^wt-local][^wt-app] | Local videos are not supported at the moment (video page FAQ)[^it-video] |
| **How you pay** | 20 free points on sign-up, about 20 minutes; then points per minute; free when someone has already translated the same video[^wt-pricing] | AI subtitles for videos without any are part of the Pro membership[^it-video] |
